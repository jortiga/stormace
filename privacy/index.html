Storm Ace Privacy Policy

Studio 3 · Effective 25 October 2026

Storm Ace is a shooter game for phones made by Studio 3. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partner handle. It covers the Android version and the iOS version.

What the game itself stores

Storm Ace has no accounts and no sign-in. Your coins, hangar upgrades, unlocked planes, best distance and your sound setting are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

Storm Ace is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: occasional full-screen ads between runs, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward, such as continuing a run or doubling your coins. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or on iPhone and iPad the IDFA, only if you allow tracking)
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Interactions with ads, plus app diagnostics and performance data

Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. If you are in the EEA, the UK or another region where consent is required, the game shows Google's consent form and asks for your choice before showing personalised ads. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time in your device's settings. On Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by phone). On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (after Google's consent form, where that applies) before the advertising identifier can be used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still see ads, but they are not personalised with that identifier.

Children

Storm Ace is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google described above, we do not share any data.

Security

Data sent by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
