Common questions

How do I play?

  • Drag anywhere to fly; your guns fire on their own
  • Bombs drop by themselves on the nearest ships, SAM sites and flak guns
  • Pick 1 of 3 upgrade cards every time you level up

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Storm Ace has no accounts or cloud saves, so progress can't be moved to another phone, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Why are there ads?

Storm Ace is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: occasional full-screen ads between runs, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward, such as continuing a run or doubling your coins. They keep the game free.

How do I change my ad choices?

If you are in a region where consent is required, the game asks before showing personalised ads. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time. On Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by phone). On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Does it work offline?

Yes, Storm Ace plays offline. Ads only load when you are connected.
