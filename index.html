About the game

Take off into an endless, storm-torn sky. Your warplane fires on its own: drag to fly, dodge and pick your targets.

TWO WEAPONS, TWO JOBS

  • Guns shoot down enemy planes
  • Bombs drop on their own onto everything below: SAM sites and flak guns on the islands, gunboats, destroyers and submarines at sea
  • Subs dive out of reach, so catch them while they surface

BUILD A NEW ACE EVERY RUN

  • Collect XP and level up mid-flight, then pick 1 of 3 upgrade cards
  • Laser Lance, Railgun, Missile Barrage, Homing Rockets and more
  • Supply crates give free picks, and every boss drops a relic

A BOSS EVERY SECTOR

  • Sky Fortress, Iron Leviathan, Iron Bastion and the Deep Kraken
  • Each sector flies over new waters, and the enemy gets tougher every time

HANGAR

  • Spend the coins you earn on permanent upgrades
  • Unlock new planes: the Sparrow, the Sky Galleon and the Gyro Hornet
